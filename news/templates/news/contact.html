Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit news to NextMedia?
You can submit news tips by emailing us at contact@nextmedia.ng with the subject line "News Submission". Please include all relevant details, sources, and any supporting media.
How do I advertise on NextMedia?
For advertising inquiries, please contact us at contact@nextmedia.ng with "Advertising" in the subject line. Our team will provide you with our media kit and pricing information.
How can I report an error or issue on the website?
We appreciate your help in maintaining quality! Please use the contact form above with the subject "Report an Issue" and describe the problem in detail. Include the page URL if applicable.
Can my school partner with NextMedia?
Absolutely! We welcome partnerships with educational institutions. Contact us with "Partnership" in the subject line, and let's discuss how we can collaborate to bring quality news coverage to your community.
How long does it take to get a response?
We typically respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please indicate "URGENT" in your subject line.
How can I delete my account or data?
To delete your account or request data deletion, please email us at privacy@nextmedia.ng. We'll process your request in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
